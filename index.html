Privacy Policy

Instagram AirSync Connector for DevRev

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Instagram AirSync Connector ("Connector") accesses and processes Instagram data to synchronize supported social interactions with DevRev.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the Connector may process the following information:

  • Instagram account IDs and usernames.
  • Post and Reel IDs, captions, links, and timestamps.
  • Comments, replies, commenter IDs, usernames, and timestamps.
  • Supported Instagram messages and conversation information.
  • Media attachments associated with supported messages, where available.

3. How We Use Information

We process authorized information to:

  • Synchronize supported Instagram interactions with DevRev.
  • Organize comments and messages into the configured DevRev records or tickets.
  • Allow authorized users to review and respond to supported interactions.
  • Maintain synchronization state and troubleshoot integration issues.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Information synchronized by the Connector may be stored and processed within DevRev and the infrastructure used to operate the integration. Access should be limited according to applicable permissions and security controls.

Data may be processed by service providers involved in delivering the service, subject to applicable agreements and requirements. Actual storage locations, retention periods, and sharing practices depend on the service configuration and the provider's policies.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained according to the applicable service configuration, retention practices, and legal requirements. Disconnecting Instagram does not necessarily delete information that was already synchronized to DevRev.

6. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of Instagram information processed through this Connector, contact the privacy contact listed below. Please identify the connected account and describe the information you want deleted. Requests will be reviewed and handled according to applicable policies and legal requirements.

You can also revoke the application's access through the relevant Meta or Instagram account settings. Revoking access may stop future data access but does not automatically delete previously synchronized data.

7. Security

Appropriate technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information processed by the Connector. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Instagram services are provided by Meta. Your use of Instagram is also subject to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies. DevRev data processing is subject to the applicable DevRev terms and privacy policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the Connector, data practices, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
